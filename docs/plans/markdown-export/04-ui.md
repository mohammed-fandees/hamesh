folder or site with its suggested layout, change it from a picker with a drawn thumbnail and a

## Notes

- **Canvas:** boards A and B were redrawn in place (rather than adding E and F) to the final
  design: a group list with a layout button and popover picker (A), an inline picker for one
  page (B), both beside a live preview. The new boards are written locally; the artifact service
  was unreachable when they were finished, so they are uploaded as soon as it answers.
- **Built against the canvas** in Chromium, Arabic light and dark, English light and dark: the
  dialog for everything, the picker open, the note menu, and one page. Screenshots were taken by
  a scratch spec, compared by eye, and the spec deleted.
- Two direction bugs found in those screenshots and fixed: `.zip` / `.md` and `Hamesh/` in the
  Arabic strings are wrapped in Unicode isolates (LRI…PDI); a lone LRM was not enough.
- **Counted, with consent:** a finished export counts its tool, scope, each layout drawn and
  whether it was copied (`export.*` in `src/domain/usage.ts`). The server keeps any name in the
  `area.thing[.detail]` shape, and the privacy policy describes the counts by kind, so neither
  needed a list changed (engineering rules §12).
- **Not exported:** team notes. Personal export is free; the team digest belongs to Teams plans
  (MONETIZATION.md), so the note menu offers export on the reader's own notes only.
- **Left out of the Settings row:** the accent tint the canvas used to point at the new row. It
  marked "new" for review, not a lasting style.
- Copy from the note menu shows "Copied" on the item for 0.9 s, then closes: the answer appears
  beside the control that was used.
- The UI tests live together in `tests/ui/export/`: `ExportDialog` (picker keyboard, Escape and
  outside click included), `LayoutPreview`, `useExport`, and `entry-points` (note menu, folder
  menu, Settings row).
